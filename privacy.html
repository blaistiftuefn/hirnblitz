ENGLISH

1. Who we are

“Hirnblitz Uploader” (the “App”) is a private software tool operated by the creator of the TikTok account @hirnblitz (“we”). It is used exclusively by us to upload quiz videos that we create ourselves to our own TikTok account. The App has no public users.

Contact: tobiasharter9@gmail.com

2. What data the App processes

When our own TikTok account is connected via TikTok Login Kit, the App processes only:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, and the account’s open_id;
  • basic profile information of our own account (display name), to confirm the connection;
  • the videos we upload and the public statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), to see which quiz formats perform best.

The App does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers, does not use cookies or tracking, and does not show advertising.

3. Where data is stored

All tokens and statistics are stored locally on our own computer. They are not uploaded to any server of ours, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

4. Third-party services

  • TikTok – the platform the videos are uploaded to (TikTok Privacy Policy).
  • ElevenLabs – generates the voice-over from our quiz texts. Only the quiz text is sent; it contains no personal data.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts this website. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses when you visit (GitHub Privacy Statement).

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the TikTok connection is active. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; we then delete the stored tokens and statistics.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Contact us at the address above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available on this page.
